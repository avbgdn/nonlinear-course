Nonlinear Dynamics of Chemical Systems

This course was developed by Andrey Vorobiev and Alexey Bogdanov, and taught at the Chemical Kinetics Lab of Lomonosov Moscow State University.

It is based on lectures and seminar classes given for more than ten years at the Department of Chemical Kinetics of the Faculty of Chemistry of Moscow State University, intended for senior undergraduate students as well as doctoral and master's students specialising in physical chemistry and chemical physics. Readers are assumed to be familiar with the basic concepts and models of physical chemistry and chemical kinetics.

The course consists of eight chapters. Where relevant, chapters are supplied with training problems and a list of additional literature.

Lectures

  1. 1. Introductory remarks
  2. 2. Foundations of the theory of qualitative analysis of nonlinear differential equations
  3. 3. Application of the theory. Analysis of singular points
  4. 4. Oscillating chemical reactions. Analysis of singular trajectories
  5. 5. Dynamical systems with three or more variables
  6. 6. Spatially distributed systems
  7. 7. Introduction to the theory of combustion and explosion
  8. 8. Macrokinetics of reactions in the solid state
